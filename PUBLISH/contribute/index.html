How it works

  1. 1
    You fill in the form below

    Pick where your addition belongs, paste the content, and optionally add your name so you get credit.

  2. 2
    We open a pull request for you

    Our app turns your submission into a proper change to the README and opens it for review. Your words are never lost.

  3. 3
    A maintainer reviews it

    Approved changes go live with the nightly batch. Rejected ones are closed with a reason so you can try again.
